## Documented hard rules (enforced)

1. Outer road + inner life must both be answerable.
2. Atlas golden questions must be representable in the brief.
3. Destination is never the main theme.
4. Open door / chapter ending (no sealed tragedy-only close).
5. Luminous nostalgia, not hopeless despair.
6. Ordinary people; no celebrity/villain frame.
7. Listener can inhabit the protagonist.
8. Music accompanies; does not manipulate.
9. Experience over virtuosity.
